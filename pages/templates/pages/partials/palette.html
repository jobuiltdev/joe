What is left for palette.js is search, arrow navigation and activation. Inside it, the input and list are a combobox over a listbox. The input keeps focus the whole time and aria-activedescendant points at the highlighted option, which is how a screen reader is told what is selected without focus ever leaving the field. The commands themselves come from pages/views.py, derived from the content module, so adding a project adds a command. {% endcomment %}
